## Findings

1. **Serious — Usage accuracy remains inconsistent.** §2 and prototype `totals()`, `orchUse()`, `usd()`, `why()`, `session()` and `models()`. Bind the design to R1's comparable, session-owned contributions and explicit exact zero, lower-bound zero and all-missing states. Carry the SDK provisional, partial-coverage and known-cost rules independently per metric, with a specific cause when the data provides one. Use bound-safe formatting across tiles, rows, source detail and rail. For example, `usd(0.086)` makes a lower bound read `≥$0.09`. The CLI token subtotal in Models drops the bound when a lane is missing, while the Cost subtotal adds it. The legend under the grid reports token coverage only, so a missing cost lane can appear solely as an unexplained hatch. Show the metric and source associated with each coverage gap in the detail. For Orchestrator, use derived `~` only when subtraction is valid on comparable coverage, or show the muted dash. The live example subtracts an estimated subagent cost, while the code also accepts lower-bound inputs and clamps inconsistent negative results to zero. Preserve uncertainty when the snapshot itself is provisional or partial, including SDK-only sessions with no subagents. Map the R1 F1–F10 states to the design. The ten named prototype fixtures are a different set.

2. **Serious — Bar geometry still misstates usage.** §§2, 4 and 5, prototype `.bar>i`, `agentRow()`, `srcbar()` and `limits()`. The CSS minimum of 2 px defeats a true zero-width share and makes the agy 0% limit windows visibly filled. Remove the fill floor from proportional encodings and show known zero as an empty track. Normalize SDK and CLI segments against the known subtotal within the available track and show incomplete coverage separately from the proportions. In the 300 px `live-small` render, the 79 px Cost track gives SDK 2 px and CLI 3.2 px, plus a 10 px hatch. Raw dollar flex factors sum to 0.08, so most of the track is unused and the hatch dominates. Define a truthful treatment for partial SDK and partial lane reports as well as wholly missing lanes. Keep the session denominator and hide shares for lower-bound totals as §4 specifies.

3. **Serious — Budget drops the weighted fallback contract.** §2 and prototype `session()`. Retain both weighted-fallback cases in R7.5. For a dollar budget with unavailable full and partial cost, and for an already-sticky fallback after cost becomes available, compare stage-weighted tokens against the configured fallback weighted-token limit. Name why the tile measures weighted tokens in place of cost and show representative states. General support for a `wtok` unit leaves the fallback basis and disclosure undefined. Preserve the stage's gold, warning and error mapping and the lower-bound partial-dollar path.

4. **Serious — Session and Orchestrator details still lose information.** §§3, 6–7 and prototype `models()`, `agentRow()`, `cacheHtml()`. Give the aggregate SDK category breakdown and coverage notes an explicit place, including uncached input, output, reads and creation. Label the per-model breakdown as SDK-scoped on entry to the disclosure, including single-model cases and when lanes contribute to the headline. Preserve estimate, subscription, partial pricing and unavailable-cost explanations, with the SDK subtotal in that same labelled detail. At 300/336 px the actual cache glyph and timer are removed from the model line. The row's tooltip is identity only, and the selected detail shows TTL, idle and state but omits the remaining countdown. Unknown cache is also absent from the selected detail when TTL is absent. Preserve the countdown and unavailable cause in the narrow detail. Specify the unattributed main-request model state (R3.2). Keep the SDK subtotal in Models and resolve the extra SDK total in Orchestrator detail against R4's duplication rule for SDK-only sessions.

5. **Serious — Agent interactions and ordering lose roster parity.** §§1 and 7, prototype `agents()`, `agentRow()` and click handlers. Keep existing conditional thread opening and identity normalization between subagent and background rows. The prototype toggles detail on an ID, and v2's inherited parity line covers selected state, focus return and kept-visible behavior only. Keep live-first ordering and the host's active-state coverage, including starting, waiting, queued and asking rows. Action controls must retain their current eligibility and keep usable context and focus through the request, steer, error, retry and background paths. §1 introduces Orchestrator-first ordering without a decision to change behavior. The `live` caption labels six rows as running while two are asking. Keep the labelled subagent and CLI counts from the roster and use a truthful live or split count.

6. **Serious — Rail Time and session-switch leave behavior are omitted.** §§1 and 7 and prototype `rail()`, `render()`. Restore accessible rail Time when a known duration exists, opening SESSION like the other session metrics. This is visible in `reference/live/05-running.png` as documented by the live findings and is an existing `compactSessionMetrics()` capability under the inventory's rail metric row. Preserve both halves of the session-switch transition with reduced-motion support and session-keyed detail expansion. The fixture switch resets Models state and plays enter only. Treat the compact sheet as the preserved host presentation with its existing dialog and focus behavior. In loading, gate context, roster and attention by their own readiness instead of showing the base fixture's 7% context and ten-agent roster behind Tok/Cost skeletons. Preserve the labelled context and plan-limit cue and their focus destinations.

7. **Serious — Keyboard users still lack equivalent metric detail.** §6 and prototype `time()`, `agentRow()`, `rail()`, `showTip()`. The rail's Cost accessible name says “at least about $17” in `limits`, while the dock holds $17.54 and an incomplete, estimated-cost explanation. Row focus exposes the row's identity, while individual metric causes and the full duration behind `1h26m` are reachable only by pointer. Provide an associated, keyboard-reachable route to the same full values, states and causes for rail, source split, rows and durations. Define controlled row detail and focus return, including Escape for a disclosure, blur/Escape tooltip dismissal and accessible meter values. Preserve the visible muted `--:--` from decision 5 and use a resume-specific cause only when the data identifies it. Treat absent, null and zero session duration as unknown under R8 while keeping independently known turns.

8. **Moderate — PLAN LIMITS needs complete provider-state mapping.** §5 and prototype `limits()`. Decision 13 is met by the hierarchy shown in `limits-300-dark.png` and `limits-400-dark.png`: Claude and Codex each show two windows, agy shows Gemini and Claude/GPT groups with two windows each, and Gemini shows a local `usage unavailable` line. Decision 15 is reflected by agy's available 0% and 1%/3% figures in `lanes` and `live-small`. Keep the current provider labels, owner status, source, refresh notice and full reset time including date/zone when available. Handle an absent or partial window/reset and provider warning or limit independently of the numerical percentage. The rail prototype only looks at percentages and explicit limit, so it can miss owner warnings and can color the cue differently from the dock. Sample resets are fixture values. The startup reader issue stays with its separate bugfix.

9. **Moderate — Width claim needs to match the rendered content.** §8 and prototype `.nm small` and fixed numeric columns. Document the ellipsis and full-value access strategy, including long values, two accuracy glyphs, hours and narrow focus outlines. A 360 px live render shows the context warning and countdown but truncates the Orchestrator model to `Fable …`, despite §8's full model-line claim. Can SDK subagent stats support subtraction on the same basis, or should a metric use the decision 12 dash? Use an SDK-normalized total once and retain per-metric causes.

- Which providers expose each window, model-group name, owner warning/limit state, account identifier and full reset timestamp on the existing projection? agy's two groups were confirmed manually in decision 15. Use an unavailable state where the provider or individual field has no data.
- Map the design to the real shared APIs for `ptah-stat`, `ptah-timer`, `ptah-ring`, `ptah-countdown`, `ptah-meter`, table and disclosure. Validate the identified size, segment and value-format gaps in the Electron dock, rail and compact sheet. Keep data boundaries host-neutral.
