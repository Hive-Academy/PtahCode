### Q2. Where and why the pending question is aborted at turn end, and why sync vs async differ

1. **Exact abort chain at turn end**:
   - Because `request_user_input_async` immediately returns `{"accepted":true}` to the model, Codex finishes its turn right after emitting `item/completed` (`request_user_input_async`) and emits `turn/completed`.
   - `CodexEventTranslator.translate` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-event-translator.ts:439-477`) translates `turn/completed` into a `CodexRuntimeEvent` with `type: 'terminal'`, `status: 'completed'`.
   - `RuntimeContext.admitEvent` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:623-641`) handles `event.type === 'terminal'` by calling `this.finishActiveTurn()` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:640`).
   - `RuntimeContext.finishActiveTurn()` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:686-688`) clears `this.activeTurnId` and calls **`this.turnController.abort()`** (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:687`).
   - Because `RuntimeContext.askAsyncUserInput` passed `const signal = this.turnController.signal` to `this.options.requestUserInput(request, signal)` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:913, 916`):
     1. `AskUserQuestionService.awaitQuestionResponse` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/permission/ask-user-question.service.ts:216-221`) runs `onAbort`, calls `resolveHostAttention()`, deletes the pending entry from `PendingResponseRegistry` (`line 218`), and resolves `null` (`line 219`).
     2. `AskUserQuestionService.handleAskUserQuestion` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/permission/ask-user-question.service.ts:128-136`) logs `[SdkPermissionHandler] AskUserQuestion aborted` and returns `{ behavior: 'deny', message: 'Question request was aborted' }`.
     3. `createCodexChatUserQuestions` (`libs/backend/rpc-handlers/src/lib/chat/codex-host/codex-chat-user-questions.ts:71-82`) sees `signal.aborted && (result === null || result.behavior !== 'allow')`, calls `deps.permissionHandler.cleanupPendingPermissions(sessionId)` (`line 73`), and resolves `{}` (`line 82`).
     4. `RuntimeContext.askAsyncUserInput`'s `.then(...)` callback (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:917-927`) checks `if (signal.aborted)` (`line 924`) and calls `this.warnDenialFeedback('turn-ended', 'user-input')` (`line 925`), logging `Codex user answer was not delivered.` with `{ code: 'codex-user-input-undelivered', cause: 'turn-ended' }` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:956-968`).

2. **Why aborting at turn end is right for synchronous `request_user_input` (`item/tool/requestUserInput`) and wrong for `request_user_input_async`**:
   - Synchronous `item/tool/requestUserInput` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:175-195`) is a blocking JSON-RPC server request: the turn stays suspended inside `codex app-server` until the client responds. If the turn terminates before a response is sent, the turn was interrupted/cancelled and the blocking server request is dead.
   - Asynchronous `request_user_input_async` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-event-translator.ts:543`, `libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:885-948`) is non-blocking by contract: the tool returns `{"accepted":true}` to the model immediately, and the model ends its turn (`turn/completed`) to wait for the user's reply. Binding `askAsyncUserInput` to `this.turnController.signal` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:913`) guarantees that `finishActiveTurn()` (`line 687`) aborts the question milliseconds after it is displayed. An async user question must remain pending across turn completion and only abort when the `RuntimeContext` itself is closed (`RuntimeContext.close` at `libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:535-559`).

### Q3. How an answer to an async question should reach Codex after the turn that asked it has already ended, and what `@openai/codex-sdk` supports

1. **What `@openai/codex-sdk` supports**:
   - Inspected `node_modules/@openai/codex-sdk/package.json` (`version: "0.165.0"`) and `node_modules/@openai/codex-sdk/dist/index.d.ts:1-291`.
   - `@openai/codex-sdk` is a minimal wrapper around `codex exec` (`Codex.startThread`, `Codex.resumeThread`, `Thread.run`, `Thread.runStreamed` at `node_modules/@openai/codex-sdk/dist/index.d.ts:240-290`). Its `ThreadItem` union (`lines 92-99`) and `ThreadEvent` union (`lines 198-208`) have **no** `request_user_input`, `request_user_input_async`, `turn/steer`, or interactive question/approval protocol support.

2. **How Ptah's Codex App Server runtime delivers the answer — and the two defects that break post-turn delivery**:
   - Ptah's Codex integration (`libs/backend/codex-runtime`) speaks JSON-RPC 2.0 to `codex app-server` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-protocol-methods.ts:183-226`), which supports `turn/steer` (`CODEX_CLIENT_REQUEST_METHODS.turnSteer`, `line 194`) when a turn is active and `turn/start` (`CODEX_CLIENT_REQUEST_METHODS.turnStart`, `line 192`) on the existing `threadId` when the previous turn has ended.
   - `RuntimeContext.deliverAnswer` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:975-998`) formats the answer text via `formatUserInputAnswers` (`lines 1000-1011`) and executes:
     - `if (this.activeTurnId) { await this.steer({ text }); return; }` (`lines 982-984`),
     - `await this.start({ text })` (`line 988`).
   - **Defect F4-B (Unconsumed follow-up turn when `RuntimeContext.deliverAnswer` calls `this.start({ text })` directly)**:
     - When the turn that emitted `request_user_input_async` ends (`turn/completed`), `streamCodexChatTurn` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/codex-app-server/codex-chat-turn-stream.ts:150-164, 343-370`) exits its `for await (const event of session.context.events())` generator loop, and `ChatStreamBroadcaster.streamEventsToWebview` (`libs/backend/rpc-handlers/src/lib/chat/streaming/chat-stream-broadcaster.service.ts:338-524`) exits its `for await` loop.
     - Calling `this.start({ text })` directly inside `RuntimeContext.deliverAnswer` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:988`) starts a new turn on `codex app-server` **without** `CodexChatAdapter` running `this.turn(session, { text })` (`streamCodexChatTurn`) or publishing the stream to `ChatStreamBroadcaster.streamEventsToWebview` via `this.ports.publish(session.id, stream)` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/codex-app-server/codex-chat-adapter.ts:650-682`, `libs/backend/rpc-handlers/src/lib/chat/codex-host/codex-chat-host-ports.ts:556-557`). Any events produced by that follow-up turn sit in `RuntimeContext.eventQueue` with no consumer and never reach the webview.
     - By contrast, `CodexChatApprovalSink` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/codex-app-server/codex-chat-approval-sink.ts:30-94` and `libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/codex-app-server/codex-chat-adapter.ts:634-682`) already solves this exact problem for post-turn approval denial feedback: when `session.turnId` is active (`codex-chat-adapter.ts:640`), it calls `session.context.steer({ text, expectedTurnId: session.turnId })`; when `session.turnId` is `undefined` (`codex-chat-adapter.ts:642`), it calls `this.publish(session, { text })` (`codex-chat-adapter.ts:650-682`), which acquires the driver lease, runs `streamCodexChatTurn`, and hands the new `AsyncIterable<StreamEvent>` to `this.ports.publish(session.id, stream)`.
   - **Defect F4-C (`ChatStreamBroadcaster.streamEventsToWebview` closes the `CodexChatAdapter` session at turn end on resumed turns)**:
     - In `ChatStreamBroadcaster.streamEventsToWebview`'s `finally` block (`libs/backend/rpc-handlers/src/lib/chat/streaming/chat-stream-broadcaster.service.ts:457-469`), `await this.sdkAdapter.endSessionIfTokenMatches(sessionId, recordToken, endCause)` is called whenever the turn stream finishes.
     - On the initial `chat:start` turn, `ChatSessionService.launchSdkSession` (`libs/backend/rpc-handlers/src/lib/chat/session/chat-session.service.ts:1756, 1790-1795`) passes `tempSessionId` (`tabId`) as `sessionId`, so `CodexChatAdapter.getSessionToken(tabId)` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/codex-app-server/codex-chat-adapter.ts:322`, keyed by `threadId`) returns `null`, accidentally sparing the session on turn 1. On any subsequent turn (`resumeSession` or `publish(session.id, stream)`), `sessionId` is `threadId`, so `getSessionToken(threadId)` returns the live token and `endSessionIfTokenMatches` closes the `TrackedSession` (`session.context.close()`) as soon as that turn completes (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/codex-app-server/codex-chat-adapter.ts:303-319`) — which would abort any `request_user_input_async` asked on turn 2+.

## F4 fix design

1. **Decouple `request_user_input_async` from `this.turnController.signal` in `RuntimeContext` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts`)**:
   - Add a context-lifetime `private readonly contextController = new AbortController();` on `RuntimeContext` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:81-96`) and abort `this.contextController` only inside `RuntimeContext.close()` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:535-559`).
   - In `RuntimeContext.askAsyncUserInput` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:885-948`), pass `const signal = this.contextController.signal;` instead of `this.turnController.signal` (`line 913`) to `this.options.requestUserInput(request, signal)` (`line 916`).
   - Keep `this.turnController.signal` for synchronous `handleServerRequest` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:186`).

2. **Route post-turn async question answers through `CodexChatAdapter.publish` so the follow-up turn is streamed to the webview (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts`, `libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/codex-app-server/codex-chat-adapter.ts`)**:
   - Add an optional callback `deliverUserInputAnswer?: (input: { threadId: string; turnId: string; itemId: string; text: string }) => Promise<void>` to `CodexContextOptions` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:55-68`).
   - In `RuntimeContext.deliverAnswer` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:975-998`), if `this.options.deliverUserInputAnswer` is provided, delegate to `await this.options.deliverUserInputAnswer({ threadId: event.threadId, turnId: event.turnId, itemId: event.itemId, text })`; otherwise fall back to the existing `steer` / `start` logic.
   - In `CodexChatAdapter.open` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/codex-app-server/codex-chat-adapter.ts:365-430`) and `CodexChatAdapter.deliver` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/codex-app-server/codex-chat-adapter.ts:634-682`), wire `deliverUserInputAnswer` onto the session (alongside `CodexChatApprovalSink`):
     - If `session.turnId` is active (`codex-chat-adapter.ts:640`), call `await session.context.steer({ text, expectedTurnId: session.turnId })`.
     - If `session.turnId` is `undefined` (the turn that asked `request_user_input_async` has already completed), call `await this.publish(session, { text })` (`codex-chat-adapter.ts:650-682`), which acquires the driver lease, runs `this.turn(session, { text })` (`streamCodexChatTurn`), and publishes the resulting `AsyncIterable<StreamEvent>` via `this.ports.publish(session.id, stream)` (`libs/backend/rpc-handlers/src/lib/chat/codex-host/codex-chat-host-ports.ts:556-557`).
   - In `CodexChatAdapter.getSessionToken` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/codex-app-server/codex-chat-adapter.ts:321-323`), keep `TrackedSession` alive across turns (matching `chat:start` behavior) or return `null` from `getSessionToken` while `session.context` is healthy so `ChatStreamBroadcaster.streamEventsToWebview`'s `finally` block (`libs/backend/rpc-handlers/src/lib/chat/streaming/chat-stream-broadcaster.service.ts:459-468`) does not close the Codex `RuntimeContext` at single-turn completion.

3. **Close the async race in `HostAttentionCoordinator` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/permission/host-attention-coordinator.ts`)**:
   - Track a `pendingKeys = new Map<string, number>()` (key -> generation) in `HostAttentionCoordinator` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/permission/host-attention-coordinator.ts:56-62`) before `await this.metadata.get(input.sessionId)` in `issueRequest` (`line 265`).
   - In `resolve(kind, requestId)` (`lines 124-134`) and `resolveSession(sessionId)` (`lines 136-152`), delete any matching entry from `pendingKeys`.
   - After `await this.metadata.get(...)` returns in `issueRequest` (`line 265`), verify `this.pendingKeys.get(key) === generation` before writing to `this.active` (`line 277`) or emitting `upsert` (`line 285`).

### Q5. Trace `harness:start-new-project` to session start, what it returns, what the renderer does after "Continue", why no chat tab opens, and Claude vs Codex comparison

## Decisions

1. **Keep `request_user_input_async` alive across turn completion (`RuntimeContext.contextController`) rather than suppressing `turn/completed`**:
   - `codex app-server` genuinely finishes its turn after `request_user_input_async` (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-event-translator.ts:439-477, 540-555`). Keeping the turn lifecycle accurate while scoping `askAsyncUserInput`'s `AbortSignal` to `RuntimeContext` close (`libs/backend/codex-runtime/src/lib/codex-context.ts:535-559, 913`) preserves `<ptah-question-card>` in both `ProposalWorkspacePage` (`libs/frontend/onboarding/src/lib/shared/details-log.component.ts:80`) and normal chat (`libs/frontend/chat/src/lib/components/templates/chat-view.component.html:182-194`).
2. **Reuse `CodexChatAdapter.publish` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/codex-app-server/codex-chat-adapter.ts:650-682`) for post-turn `request_user_input_async` answer delivery**:
   - This mirrors `CodexChatApprovalSink` (`libs/backend/agent-sdk/src/lib/codex-app-server/codex-chat-approval-sink.ts:30-94`) and ensures that the new turn triggered by the user's answer is streamed through `streamCodexChatTurn` and `ChatStreamBroadcaster.streamEventsToWebview` (`libs/backend/rpc-handlers/src/lib/chat/streaming/chat-stream-broadcaster.service.ts:338-524`).
3. **Sync `TabManagerService.tabs()` into `CanvasStore` in `OrchestraCanvasComponent` (`libs/frontend/canvas/src/lib/orchestra-canvas.component.ts:297-301`) in addition to `backToChat` (`libs/frontend/onboarding/src/lib/proposal/proposal-workspace.page.ts:154-156`)**:
   - Auto-adopting new workspace tabs into `CanvasStore` without changing `featureView` ensures that whether the user clicks `"Back to chat"`, clicks the top-bar Chat button, or clicks `"Open in Chat"`, the `'New Project'` session tile is present in `CanvasRailComponent` (`"Sessions"`) and mounted on the Canvas board.
