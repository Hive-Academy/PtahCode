## Decisions

- Keep SDK parent input acceptance asynchronous after a safety stop. The alternative, waiting for `streamInput` acknowledgment in the output iterator, can create a circular wait. The stream waits only until the stop and push are started; this is reversible and covered by the pending-ack spec.
- Treat a `SEND_TIMEOUT` as unknown acceptance and avoid retry. The durable record and notice route remain available; this is reversible if a future SDK contract can distinguish rejection from delayed acceptance.

## Remaining gaps

- No Ptah Dev, build, serve, package, or live rerun was allowed. Round 7 must prove that the parent's first reply sees the Ptah handoff before attributing an Agent interruption, and that the Codex warning appears before a future stop when prediction fires early.
- A same-turn SDK warning/stop cannot give the child a notes window. The stop path writes/merges inferred notes and pushes the full handoff instead. The 110,000-limit warning-before-stop child notes update and denial burst were not exercised here.
- The four timeout events cannot be individually correlated because the supplied sanitized log omits their lines. No claim is made that the SDK acknowledged those specific messages.
