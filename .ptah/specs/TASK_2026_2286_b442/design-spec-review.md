# Design review

Verdict: REVISE

## Blocking

1. **Keep the full Day breakdown at every supported width** (§§8a, 12, 13 Batch 6). The spec hides Top model below 640 px without moving the value anywhere. The wide layout itself triggers this rule. At the 1500 preset the Breakdown table content was about 584 px wide and the column disappeared. Put the model and its full label in the Day cell at narrower widths or give the table room for all five columns. The prototype also renders only ten sample days and hardcodes their top model (prototype/index.html:337–340). Make the fixture demonstrate the full range with per-day model values. The current page passes all days through and the row builder maps the range in reverse order (usage-dashboard.component.html:120–125, usage-table-rows.ts:127–156, usage-breakdown-table.component.ts:167–231).
2. **Preserve the chart's keyboard and unknown-cost behavior** (§§6a, 11–12, 13 Batch 3). The spec describes hover and an image label without specifying the focusable day targets, left/right navigation, focused-day marker, or the live total and per-provider readout. The prototype has zero focusable day targets and a mouse-only tooltip. Carry forward the current hover and focus parity, idle prompt, lower-bound and fully unpriced readings at both day and provider level, and cost-only hatch behavior. Keep both metric formatters and all observed provider series. The four names in the spec can illustrate the fixture, but a fixed four-series implementation drops any additional provider. Demonstrate keyboard use and partial/unpriced Cost versus Tokens in the prototype (stacked-bar-chart.component.html:19–29, :82–161, stacked-bar-chart.component.ts:184–208, :214–260, :278–292, usage-dashboard.component.ts:118–142).
3. **Give the merged Cost share card a parity contract** (§§6b, 12, 13 Batch 4). Preserve the complete provider summary, including zero-cost providers and individual providers folded into Other in the donut. Keep it available in Model mode too. Preserve visible provider token totals and the cost denominator, exact no-price and partial-price titles, lower-bound values for partly priced providers, fully unpriced rows outside the arc limit, and the visible exclusion/lower-bound explanation. Keep the legend's keyboard access and accessible row labels when reorganizing it. A single title string cannot substitute for those behaviors. State which rows represent slices and which represent individual providers, and where the existing summary/row hooks live in each mode. The prototype's Model button leaves Provider selected and the list unchanged (prototype/index.html:207–215, :325–328). Make both modes functional with priced, partly priced and unpriced fixtures (usage-cost-summary.component.ts:12–27, :36–54, usage-cost-donut.component.ts:15–50, :61–77, usage-cost-donut.geometry.ts:18–41).

## Minor

- **Chart/table balance.** At wide content width the chart fills its card, which addresses the requested chart-row dead area, but the shorter Top sessions card leaves about 170 px of blank space below it beside Breakdown. Light gridlines and the smallest colored marks are faint. The light daily-chart SVG and donut do not receive the inset edge applied to HTML bars. In the prototype's white-letter marks, Anthropic, Codex and Unknown are approximately 3.12:1, 3.20:1 and 2.54:1 against their fills. The real vendor marks and redundant row text can address the placeholder issue. Document which graphics carry information and check resolved theme contrast for them.
- **Handoff names.** Targeted `rg` searches confirmed `ptahSurface` (including `inset` and `md`), `ptahBadge`, `ptahButton`, `ptah-section-header`, `ptah-provider-mark`, `ptah-ring` (including `centerOnly`, `ariaLabel`, `row`), `ptah-meter`, `ptah-sparkline`, `ptah-table`, `ptahNumeric`, `gap-ptah-group`, `gap-ptah-field`, `gap-ptah-heading`, `gap-ptah-controls`, `gap-ptah-label`, `rounded-ptah-chip`, `text-base-content-muted`, `text-state-{err,need,done}`, `--ptah-meter-track`, `--ptah-gold`, and `--surface-border[-strong]`. Definitions are under `libs/frontend/ui/src/lib` and the Electron webview's `tailwind.config.js` and `src/styles.css`. No existing `ptah-usage-cost-share` selector was found, so label it as a proposed component or use the two existing components. No `formatUsdPrecise` exists, as §2 anticipates. The existing date helper is `formatDayShort`, not `shortDay`. Use `var(--ptah-meter-track)` where §6b currently says `ptah-meter-track`. `ptah-focus` is a Tailwind outline color/offset key with a companion `ptah-focus-width` key, not a standalone class. Use `text-base-content-muted` if the inline `muted` references are intended as production classes.

## Proposed removals (user decision)

- **Top model at narrow Breakdown widths** (§8a). Hiding the column without a replacement removes a value available today, including at roughly 1500 px page width under the proposed grid. Keep the information in a responsive row or obtain explicit approval (usage-breakdown-table.component.ts:185–188, :226–228).
- **Always-available By provider information and accessible legend** (§6b). Replacing the provider summary with a model list suppresses provider information until the user changes modes. Removing token totals, denominator and cost tooltips, individual provider rows, keyboard-reachable legend context, or the visible unpriced notice also changes capability. Preserve them in the combined card or obtain explicit approval (usage-dashboard.component.html:99–109, usage-cost-summary.component.ts:12–54, usage-cost-donut.component.ts:36–50).
- **Repository information behind hover or a closed disclosure** (§9). The full incomplete-coverage warning is visible in the current page, and all recorded worktrees are immediately visible. The badge title and closed-by-default disclosure for four or more paths reduce that visibility. Keep the warning readable and the list expanded by default, or obtain explicit approval for the presentation change (usage-dashboard.component.html:139–147).

## Lane-introduced constraints

The request delegates visual design. These specific prescriptions come from the spec and are design choices unless separately adopted.

- `lane-proposed` (§§0–1, 13, Decisions) Mandate a surface or nested tile for every block, a 24/16 px gap system, 16 px default padding, container queries exclusively, and a blanket CSS ban on `overflow-x-auto`. The user requested the no-scroll outcome. The exact ratios, minimums, stretch behavior and breakpoint values for charts, tables, cards and repository grids are also proposed. Existing KPI cutoffs are retained behavior.
- `lane-proposed` (§§2–4) Standardize title levels and type sizes, impose an 11 px caption floor, lock the palette/font/token set, prohibit provider-colored text, and specify precise rounding/cutoffs, currency symbols, compact/full number presentation, and meta-line spacing. The particular WCAG target, provider-edge treatment and numeric verification are proposed acceptance criteria. The existing visual system and accessible behavior still apply.
- `lane-proposed` (§5) Use flex-grow for final rows, two-line clamps, 28 px marks, 56 px rings, “Selected” shorthand, status glyphs, neutral mappings for less certain states, a count badge and two-line chip rows, and ghost-only footer actions. The two-mini-row cap and source/action semantics are existing behavior.
- `lane-proposed` (§6) Prescribe 64% day slots, 3–4 px corners, a 1 px segment stroke, 220 px minimum plot height, fixed provider order and tick spacing, a 148 px donut with 2 px gaps, title/peak copy, maximum-relative list bars and minimum 1% widths. Consolidating headings and lists is a design proposal subject to parity. Provider-tinted model ramps in §12 are optional and also `lane-proposed`.
- `lane-proposed` (§§7–8) Require the Cost quality caption and swatches, a 10 px segmented composition bar, gold mixing and pattern choices, particular divider and cell rules, table summary copy, concentration strip, chip size, bar geometry and minimums, exact narrow-width folding/hiding cutoffs, and the busiest-day subtitle. The full-width strip, more table space, visual encodings and no horizontal scroll come from the user.
- `lane-proposed` (§§9–11) Derive the repository name from its path, add the repository glyph and cost-share bar, shorten the warning into a badge, switch to a two-up repository grid, and disclose worktrees by count with an open-by-default cutoff of three. New constraints on accessible section markup, tooltip implementation, focus styling and motion are handoff choices where they go beyond preserving current behavior and the project primitives.
- `lane-proposed` (§13) Require the nine-batch implementation order, the proposed test ID names and the particular screenshot width matrix. Scoped verification and Electron-first visual review come from project guidance.

## What was rendered and checked

- Opened the in-workspace `file://` prototype with `ptah_browser_navigate` and inspected full-page `ptah_browser_screenshot` output in dark and light at 900 px and about 1500 px content width. The 900 px value was the prototype stage inside the visible 1500 px browser window. The wide stage resolved to about 1485–1500 px with the scrollbar and capture mode. I checked the rendered order, card backgrounds, chart height, table columns/bars/marks, account titles and repository formatting against the brief and spec. No screenshot files were written.
- **Full-width row passes.** Both themes and widths show all four Cost quality cells beneath the entire chart band. At 900 the charts stack above the strip. At wide width the charts sit side by side and the Daily usage plot grows to about 292 px versus 220 px at 900.
- **Table design is visible.** The tables stack with full-width backgrounds at 900 and split into wider session/narrower breakdown cards at the wide preset. The populated sample's page and table widths showed no horizontal overflow in either mode. I saw no cut text in the default-width sample labels. The wide Day view suppresses Top model, and the short fixture gives insufficient evidence for long labels, partial headings and large values.
- **Whole-page redesign is visible.** Plan limits sample titles fit and the ring no longer repeats the provider title. Ring and body still repeat values such as 95%, and short Plan cards have some spare space above aligned footers. Repository shows compact and full separated token counts, formatted currency, coverage and worktree disclosure. Body labels were generally readable in both themes. Light plot gridlines and small graphics had the weakest contrast.
- Used browser DOM inspection to exercise the theme/width controls, usage state switches, Breakdown modes, Cost share Model and Details buttons, and to measure overflow and chart geometry. Used the workspace UI/UX designer and Angular frontend skill files and native `rg` for source/identifier checks. Review was limited to the static design artifacts and existing code. No app, build, Nx, verify or tests ran.
