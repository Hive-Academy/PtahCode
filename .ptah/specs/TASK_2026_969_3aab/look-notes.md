# Form control look notes

The temporary repro is `.ptah/tmp/form-controls.html`. It loads renderer CSS compiled from `styles.css` with the project Tailwind config. The screenshots are `.ptah/tmp/ptah-glass-after.png` and `.ptah/tmp/ptah-glass-light-after.png`. The original stylesheet was changed before the first browser capture, so there is no trustworthy before screenshot. The cause statements below use the original source rules and the after computed styles.

## Defects

1. **Workspace name focus.** The global glass `.input:focus` and `:focus-visible` rules add an inner outline and shadow. The family rule also adds an outline. The field box and rail add two more marks. `ptah-field.css` now gives the field box the keyboard outline and removes the nested outline and shadow. The rail is reserved for error and busy. In the dark repro the focused box has a 2 px outline. Its input has `outline-style: none` and `box-shadow: none`. In Electron, tab to Workspace name and inspect the edge and both inside corners.
2. **Search sessions.** WebKit supplies a native search cancel button beside the field clear button. `ptah-field.css` hides the native cancel and decoration controls only for search inputs in a field body (`.ptah-field__body`). A standalone `ptahInput type="search"` keeps the native clear action, because no owner supplies a replacement. The field reset also removes the inner borders that made vertical lines near the slots. In Electron, enter a query. Check that one clear action appears and that the icon and action have no divider lines.
3. **Provider and Region.** `.ptah-field__body` is flex, but the rich select host had content width. `ptah-select.css` now grows the host and moves the chevron to the far end. In a floating field the trigger pads the top for the label (1.25 rem top, 0.375 rem bottom), so the chevron sat about 7 px below the box center. A negative top margin on the chevron (pad-bottom minus pad-top) moves it to the box center, the same line as the clear action and a native select chevron. The trigger in the repro is 582 px wide inside a 608 px field box. In Electron, compare Provider, Region and standalone Sort order. Check label height, chevron position and vertical alignment.
4. **Model panel.** Global focus styles draw an outline and halo on the search input. `ptah-select.css` removes them from the panel search and marks its row with the focus color. `ptah-field.css` keeps the trigger focus on the outer field box only. In Electron, open Model and tab to search. Check the trigger, search row and bottom edge.
5. **Fields gallery.** `ptahFieldControl` adds DaisyUI classes but no `data-ptah-control`, so the old field reset missed it. `ptah-field.css` now resets native input, select and textarea inside the field body regardless of directive. The repro shows a 582 px control inside a 608 px box, 20 px top padding for input and select, 28 px for textarea, and textarea `resize: none`. In Electron, check Review name, Repository and Instructions for one box and separate label and value lines.

## Focus contrast

From the headless Chromium screenshots, the solid 2 px outline is 15.84:1 against the adjacent dark glass background (ring RGB 242,242,241; background 23,24,28) and 7.20:1 against the glass light background (ring RGB 110,74,0; background 246,242,252). Both exceed 3:1. The screenshot samples are outside the translucent field box, where the outline is drawn.

## Decisions

- Use the box outline as the single field focus mark. The rail still indicates error or busy. Evidence: the field owner already draws the box and the rail; drawing both on focus creates two indicators. Reversible: yes.
- Keep `ptahFieldControl` public selectors and classes. Scope the reset to `.ptah-field__body` so controls outside a field retain their existing look. Reversible: yes.

## Round 2 (user verdict: not accepted)

No browser or Electron session ran in round 2. The causes come from the CSS cascade and the templates. Lane details: `round2-field-edge.md`, `round2-select.md`, `round2-review.md`.

1. **Two edges on a focused field.** Cause: the round 1 correction itself. `.ptah-field__box` keeps its 1 px border, and at focus it added a 2 px outline with `outline-offset`. The border and the outline are two concentric lines about 3 px apart. The control reset also applied only to `:focus-visible`. Thus a focus without `:focus-visible` could keep the glass `:focus` rule of `styles.css` (outline, border color, 5 px ring) on the native control. The thin inner vertical lines are probably the edges of that control focus paint, clipped by the box. A render did not confirm this. Correction (`ptah-field.css`): the box has no outline. At focus (`:has(.ptah-field__body :focus)`, keyboard and mouse), the box border takes the focus color, and a 1 px inset band of the same color touches it. This gives one 2 px edge with no layout shift. The control reset now matches `:focus` and `:focus-visible` and removes outline, box-shadow and border. The focus rule comes after the invalid and read-only rules, so focus stays one solid edge in those states. Look at: "Name on base-200", the textarea and a select in a field. Click them and tab to them, in dark glass and `ptah-glass-light`. Check one edge and no inner lines.
2. **Select icons not at the end and moving.** Causes (`ptah-select.css`): the chevron was a flex item with `margin-inline-start: auto`. A padding rule applied only while a clear button existed, and the clear button exists only with a value. The clear button was absolutely positioned at `top: 50%` of the host, and the host grew while the panel was still in flow before positioning. A floating-field margin moved only the chevron. The trigger also kept its own end padding inside the field. Correction: one `.ptah-select__actions` group after the trigger (order: clear, then chevron), in a one-cell grid with `justify-self: end; align-self: center`. It has the inset of the text field clear action. The trigger always keeps room for the group (from `clearable`, not from the value). The panel is `position: fixed`, so opening it does not change the host height. The open state only rotates the chevron around its center. Look at: Provider (clearable) and Region. Open, close, clear and select again. The icons must not move.
3. **Native select menu.** Correction: the gallery uses `ptah-select` as the standard select (Fields "Repository", the List and detail toolbar sort, the "Page size" row). One native demo stays, labeled "Legacy ptahSelect (native menu)". New `size` input (`xs`, `sm`, `md`, control height tokens). Search is automatic: on from 8 options (`PTAH_SELECT_SEARCH_MIN_OPTIONS`), off for short lists. `searchable` forces it on or off. Keyboard and ARIA behavior did not change. No product template changed. Look at: the toolbar sort and the Page size row.
4. **Translucent panel.** Cause: `--ptah-select-panel-bg` used `--glass-fill-overlay`, which has alpha in glass themes, and the panel had a backdrop blur. Correction: `--ptah-select-panel-bg: var(--surface-3)` (a mix of two opaque base colors). The backdrop filter is removed. The edge and the shadow tokens did not change. The muted text on `surface-3` is already checked for AA in `design-tokens.spec.ts`. Look at: the open Model panel over page text, in dark glass and `ptah-glass-light`.

### Target: `ptah-select` replaces native selects

`ptah-select` is the target select of the design system. `ptahSelect` stays for existing native selects while the adoption track uses it. A later migration needs:

- A list of usages. `ptahSelect` is in 10 files of `libs/frontend/chat` (session filter bar, session organization editor, provider modals and popovers, ptah-ai settings panels). Raw `<select>` exists in about 30 more files (for example `provider-model-picker.component.ts`, `review-findings-panel.component.ts`, `task-bulk-bar.component.ts`, tasks-ui, git-ui, review-runs-ui, cron, setup wizard). Count them again before the migration.
- Forms integration: `ptah-select` already provides `NG_VALUE_ACCESSOR`. Templates that use `(change)` with `$event.target.value` on a native select must change to `[(value)]` or a forms binding.
- Option groups and disabled options mapped from `<optgroup>` / `<option disabled>`.
- Native form submission (`name` and a hidden value) where a `<form>` posts the value.
- Sizes: `xs` for toolbars, `sm` as default, `md` in forms.
- E2E selectors that use `selectOption` on a native select must change to the listbox pattern.

## Round 3 (user verdict: two defects)

No browser or Electron session ran in round 3. The causes come from the CSS cascade. Lane details: `round3-notch-label.md`, `round3-glass-edge.md`, `round3-review.md`.

1. **Floating label animation.** The user asked for a simpler notch label. Correction (`ptah-field.css`, pure CSS, no JavaScript, no keyframes, no template change): the label is absolute in `.ptah-field__body`. At rest (empty control, no focus, `ptah-select` closed and empty) it sits at the text start and the text size, centered for input and select, on the first line for textarea. On focus, with a value, with autofill, with a real placeholder, with a native select, or with `ptah-select` open or filled (`[data-open]`, `[data-ptah-filled]`), it moves to the top border and becomes small. Only focus gives it the focus color. It has an opaque fill `--ptah-field-notch-bg` (default base-100) and inline padding `--ptah-field-notch-pad-x`, which cut the border. One transition (top, size, color, fill) with `--ptah-ease-standard`. `prefers-reduced-motion` removes it. The box no longer clips (`overflow: hidden` removed), so the rail covers the padding box with the inner radius and a mask shows its bottom band. The floating control padding is removed, so the text is centered in the box. Stacked appearance, error, busy, counter, clear and slots did not change. Look at: "Name on base-200", "Instructions", "Legacy ptahSelect", "Provider", "Region", "Repository", each empty, focused and with a value. Check that the label text start stays on the text start, the border is cut cleanly at the label, and only one short movement occurs.
2. **Second border in glass only.** Causes: in glass, the `styles.css` form rules (strong edge, inset shadow, offset focus outline, 5 px halo) also applied to family controls, and the family focus rule covered only `:focus-visible`, so a mouse focus kept the glass paint. In a field and on a standalone control, the translucent glass fill painted under the translucent border and gave a second tonal line. Correction: the glass form rules of `styles.css` exclude `[data-ptah-control]`. In glass, family controls (`ptah-form-controls.css`) and a standalone `ptah-select` trigger (`ptah-select.css`) use one focus edge for mouse and keyboard focus: the border takes the focus color and a 1 px inset band touches it, with no outline and no halo, and no layout shift. The field box, the family controls and the trigger use `background-clip: padding-box` in glass only. The owner rules use `:where(:root[data-glass='on'] *)`, so the owner class stays first and the weight does not change. Non-glass themes did not change. Raw DaisyUI controls keep the glass look. Look at: in dark glass and `ptah-glass-light`, a field input, textarea, native select and `ptah-select`, and the standalone family controls, at rest, hover, mouse focus, keyboard focus, invalid and disabled. Check one edge, also at the rounded corners. Compare one non-glass theme with round 2: it must look the same.

Open points for the visual check:

- The notch fill is opaque. On a glass panel the blurred panel shows a little behind the box, so the base-100 notch can look like a small patch next to the label. If so, set `--ptah-field-notch-bg` on that panel to its opaque base (`var(--surface-1)` or `var(--surface-3)`). The gallery tint panels set base-200 and base-300.
- `--ptah-field-pad-top`, `--ptah-field-pad-bottom` and `--ptah-field-label-float-top` have no usage now. They stay declared for adopters. Remove them in a follow-up.
- Review (`round3-review.md`, CHANGES, one major finding): an open `ptah-select` gave the label the focus color without focus. The `.ptah-field:has(.ptah-select[data-open]) .ptah-field__label` color branch is removed. The panel is inside the select host, so focus in the open panel still matches `:focus-within`. The notch spec pins that every active-color rule uses `:focus-within` and no `[data-open]`.
