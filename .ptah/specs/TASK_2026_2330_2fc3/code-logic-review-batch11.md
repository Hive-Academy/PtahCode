## Five logic questions

1. **How does this fail silently?** Discovery can fail and Home looks empty. Dismiss can appear successful until passive adoption recreates the checkpoint row. Findings 2 and 3.
2. **What user action produces unexpected behavior?** Expanding A then switching to B reveals all B rows, and dismissing a focused row drops focus. Findings 6 and 7.
3. **What input data produces a wrong answer?** A completed disk-only analysis yields no row, two same-workflow completed records yield indistinguishable controls, and a needs-input record reads “Ready.” Findings 1, 8 and 9.
4. **What happens when a dependency fails?** A resumable lookup failure yields null evidence and existing records remain at their last known status. A first lookup failure provides neither rows nor actionable feedback. `wizard-rpc.service.ts:398`, `setup-run.store.ts:730`. The navigation specs stub a successful Router call, so they give no evidence for Router cancellation or failure after markSeen at `home/setup-runs-list.component.ts:163`.
5. **What is missing that the requirements never mentioned?** A focus destination after automatic DOM removal, stable per-run names in history, and durable agreement between Dismiss and passive adoption. Findings 2, 6 and 9.
