## Proposed Removals

- Automatic budget `stopSubagent` wiring, forced-stop retries and synthetic stop notices for new handoffs (B, C, E, F, G, I, J). Retain SDK and RPC explicit stop and decode historical budget-stop records.
- Forced handoff retirement, runtime-cap kill fallback and timer-driven first-launch kill (C2, C3, C5, C6, T3). Preserve normal resource cleanup and explicit cancellation.
- Automatic lane guard entry into `stop()`, guard-driven queued-message discard and false terminal emissions (L1–L4). Preserve terminal reporting for actual outcomes.
- Compaction-dwell abort and budget-stop-only interrupted-result classifier/retry advice (W4, J). Preserve controlled compaction bookkeeping and scoped cancellation explanations for actual provider abort or explicit user stop.
